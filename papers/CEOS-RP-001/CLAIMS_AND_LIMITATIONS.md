# CEOS-RP-001 — Claims and Limitations

## What this paper supports

The paper documents and formalizes the CEOS research architecture as observed and specified at its stated evidence anchors. It supports the proposition that CEOS is designed around a separation between probabilistic reasoning and deterministic execution authority, with explicit semantics for state, capability, freshness, validation, evidence, recovery, and canonical disposition.

It also establishes a research hypothesis and experimental program for comparing this integrated lifecycle against a strong incumbent composite.

## What this paper does not establish

The paper does not by itself establish:

- peer-reviewed scientific consensus;
- production deployment or runtime activation;
- end-to-end non-bypassability;
- formal verification of every proposed invariant;
- correctness of every planned mechanism;
- superiority over incumbent engineering stacks;
- commercial product-market fit;
- universal exactly-once external effects;
- immunity to prompt injection or model compromise;
- distributed/federated I6 behavior as an active production capability;
- empirical validation of all mathematical formalizations.

## Planned or theoretical mechanisms

Concepts described as future, proposed, planned, candidate, or theoretical must remain classified that way until separate implementation and evidence establish otherwise. These include, where applicable, formal Authority Attenuation Calculus, Provenance-Carrying Information Lattice, Attested Enforcement Assurance, Effect Commit Protocol, Counterfactual Policy Certification, Health Matrix formalization, and future distributed/federated semantics.

## Central falsifiable hypothesis

A fair benchmark should test whether CEOS's integrated enforcement materially improves one or more governed outcomes relative to a correctly configured incumbent composite without unacceptable regressions.

The null hypothesis remains admissible: a strong incumbent composite may achieve semantic equivalence at reasonable implementation and operational cost.

## Interpretation rule

Architectural coherence is not empirical proof. Mathematical notation is not automatically a theorem. Validation evidence applies only to the obligations and state it actually covers.
